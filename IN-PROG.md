## Development process

Before touching any code, begin the session by describing which phase is next for development, the work to be done, estimated development effort, and confidence level. Do this at the beginning of the session without waiting to be asked to do so.
If confidence is less than 90%, describe what you need in order to get it >=90%.
If anything is unclear, ask as many questions as needed (one at a time) until you are clear.
Once your confidence is >=90% and everything is clear, ask if you have approval to begin development.
If the answer is yes, develop the work agreed upon.
Continue developing until that work is complete or you run into unexpected issues.
If you run into unexpected issues, pause development and discuss with the user.
Once you complete development, describe what you did, and seek approval to update the plan file to reflect the updated status.
Once you've updated the file, await further instructions.
